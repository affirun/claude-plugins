# Thorough mode

Only when the user asks for it. Same steps, with these changes:

- **Search every platform with every plan query of every target**, and scroll each search to about **40 results** (two or three scrolls; re-run the extraction after each).
- **Account sweep:** every account with a same lead (seller, brand, factory, reviewer): open its profile and scroll its whole grid (cap 80 videos). In testing single accounts held several videos of the product (SuperDeker, Dozzzly, Nora Casa, a Douyin factory). Douyin profile: `https://www.douyin.com/user/<authorInfo.secUid>`; TikTok: `https://www.tiktok.com/@<handle>`.
- **Same-shoot hunt:** when a candidate shares the reel's setting, search for its title words and account on the other platforms; the same shoot is often posted by several sellers.
- **Snowball and cross-platform names:** new queries from same results' titles, hashtags, related searches; Chinese names that worked go to RedNote (and translated to TikTok), English names that worked go back to TikTok in variants and translated to Douyin.
- **Google Lens on every distinct scene of each target** (whole frames), plus one product crop, each with its Exact matches tab.
- **1688 once per run**, and the videos on its top matching product pages.
- **Stop at saturation, per target:** three searches or sweeps in a row with no new same lead for that target. Safety caps 40 searches and 12 sweeps per target.
- **Verify every** same and near lead with five moments (10/30/50/70/90%). Keep similar ones without the quick-mode cap of 8 (the app takes 30 candidates per session in all).
- Set `"mode": "thorough"` in `run.json`.
